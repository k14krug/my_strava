## 4. Confirmed design choices (not new proposals)

- Qualified power-derived stress preferred for trustworthy Zwift/virtual rides, computed with dated FTP; no eligibility by ride title/type alone.
- Strava outdoor *estimated power* is not the authoritative primary source. Retain underlying evidence.
- A pragmatic approximate HR-derived source is desired for rides without adequately qualified power, especially outdoor rides.
- Fitness = 42-day exponential update, Fatigue = 7-day update from daily selected stress. **Today's Form = yesterday's Fitness − yesterday's Fatigue.** Zero seed before initial usable load; early history is model-provisional. Formula coefficients are `1-exp(-1/42)` and `1-exp(-1/7)`.
- For an unscored recorded ride, **retain stress = unavailable** in activity evidence, but apply **numerical zero only to its missing model contribution**, following the Owner's accepted pragmatic Elevate-style convention. A no-record day likewise has zero *recorded* model load, not asserted rest. Do not silently rewrite P4-01's missing-stress evidence semantics.
- No future projection, fixed Form readiness/overload zones, workout prescription, subjective questionnaire, HRSS-to-PSS blanket multiplier, outdoor power reconstruction, or changes to trusted Performance.
- Owner-approved [Training State screen](../design/P4-02-training-state-screen.md): header/subtitle/info disclosure; 6-week/3-month/12-month/all-history; three selected-day values; large three-line toggleable chart, aligned stress strip; persistent selected-day ride detail; distinct trailing 7-/42-day stress and work/kJ context; new navigation and compact Home access.

## 5. Selected v1 input and source policy

For each recorded ride, preserve source candidate values separately and select **one** contribution to the modeled stress. No PSS+HRSS addition for the same ride.

**Selected source precedence:**
1. **Qualified power-derived stress** over the accepted recorded active scope, using the approved FTP effective on the ride date, P4-01 timer and gap rules. Distinguish known complete-recorded interval from independently verified whole session. Do not relabel an interval calculation as full-ride evidence; expose its scope.
2. **Usable HR-derived approximate ride load**, if power is unavailable or materially partial. A valid native HR stream with supported active duration is preferred; a source-reported measured average HR paired with a compatible **same-source reported active/moving duration** may produce an **explicitly labeled HR summary estimate even when the timestamped stream is sparse, missing, or fails the stream-specific completeness screen**. Do not claim that a summary independently verified complete session HR coverage. Retain any partial power subtotal separately; select HR or power per ride, never sum both.
3. **Qualified partial observed-power segment stress**, when no suitable HR source exists, clearly labeled an incomplete numerical contribution and with unscored scope visible.
4. **Unavailable** if neither source is defensible; zero numerical update contribution but retain an unscored-ride indicator.

**Recorded work kJ** is ALWAYS based on observed power only, separately from the stress input; never synthesize kJ from HR, estimated watts, or FIT gap correction. Preserve contributor counts, known excluded/missing seconds, record-date vs no-record-date distinction, and scope of partial totals. Do not apply power stress where FTP is unknown or source/device eligibility fails. API streams may supply eligible observed-only stress, never gap-corrected API power.

**Source-selection boundary — v1 Analyst specification (2026-10-10):** Follow P4-01 power evidence rules unchanged. For HR, distinguish **sampled-stream coverage** from **activity-level approximate HR summary evidence**. Strict timestamp/gap/coverage tests apply only when claiming sufficient recorded stream coverage; they MUST NOT automatically veto a valid *separate* same-source mean-HR plus duration summary. The summary path must have documented measured HR and a defensible same-source moving/active duration (for Strava API, `has_heartrate` + `average_heartrate` with positive `moving_time` no greater than `elapsed_time`; for TCX, compatible single-lap mean HR and `TotalTimeSeconds` with explicitly unverified pause/active semantics). Reject missing/nonfinite/invalid fields and conflicting eligible sources; do not derive movement from unsupported CSV duration units, assume elapsed span equals active duration, invent samples, or use Elevate scores as inputs. Label the fallback **HR summary estimate; completeness and pause treatment unverified** with method/source/version and retained stream rejection. It is an approximate *ride stress* for trend modeling, not a claim of fully measured ride effort. Do not add power+HR stress for one ride. If none qualifies, stress remains unavailable in evidence and adds numerical zero to the model. Do not add a broadly trusted partial-HR class without separate review. Acceptance must demonstrate both correct source eligibility and credible training-load response to independently qualified real rides; numerical self-consistency alone is insufficient.

## 6. HRSS-style v1 method — Owner-approved approach, Analyst-specified calculation

**Owner-approved approximate assumptions for v1:** resting HR **58 bpm**, maximum HR approximately **158 bpm**, and threshold HR **143 bpm** from the 85%-of-heart-rate-reserve fallback. Use them as **explicit modeling assumptions for older rides without accepted dated HR evidence**, not as historical measurements. If appropriate verified date-effective HR settings exist for a ride, use those instead. Preserve provenance, algorithm version, assumed/dated status, and the applied time scope; support recalculation if settings change.

Implement a **simple, deterministic, threshold-normalized TRIMP-like HRSS** inspired by Elevate:
- Use recorded heart-rate information and a source-reported activity duration on a defensible common scope. Prefer a valid timestamped HR stream when stream coverage is demonstrably good; **otherwise, accept a clearly labeled same-source measured HR summary with a compatible reported moving/active duration under §5**, even if a defective stream exists. Exclude verified pauses where known and disclose unknown pause treatment. Reject invalid/missing HR and duration; never manufacture missing HR samples or silently infer active time.
- A simple average-HR-reserve/duration implementation is adequate for v1 (Elevate's published implementation itself derives TRIMP from average HR reserve and moving time); no elaborate calibration or HR lag compensation. Derive and retain the selected mean HR, activity duration, HR setting basis, threshold assumption, method version, and source reference. Handle invalid HR settings/recording by returning unavailable.
- **Analyst-specified reproducible v1 recipe:** 100 HR stress points correspond to one hour at the assumed threshold. With measured mean HR and active seconds on a common valid scope, define `r = (mean_hr - resting_hr) / (max_hr - resting_hr)` and `r_thr = (threshold_hr - resting_hr) / (max_hr - resting_hr)`. Compute `HRSS = 100 * (active_seconds / 3600) * (r * exp(1.92*r)) / (r_thr * exp(1.92*r_thr))`. This uses a **fixed coefficient 1.92 as a modeling constant**, not as an assumption about the rider's sex/gender, and is *inspired by*, not guaranteed identical to, Elevate across configurations. Validate finite inputs, `resting_hr < mean_hr < max_hr`, `0 < r_thr < 1`, and positive active duration; invalid/insufficient evidence means unavailable. Record coefficient, HR source/coverage, duration choice, parameter context and method version. Synthetic verification should check one hour exactly at 143 bpm gives about 100 HRSS (before any rounding).
- **Historical applicability (Owner-approved):** Deliberately apply the 58/158/143 figures as a *stable retrospective assumption set* on older HR recordings lacking accepted dated settings. Do **not** assert these were measured at each historical date, or apply them to alter historical FTP or source evidence. When genuine applicable dated settings later become available, recalculate with clear version/provenance change.

Neither the Elevate CSV nor original/mockup data should be used as production data, model seeds, or checked-in fixtures. **Owner privacy exception, 2026-10-09:** the exact unchanged `data/reference/elevate/fitness_trend_export.2026.10.9-15.58.52.csv` is authorized as a committed permanent external reference dataset (496,966 bytes; SHA-256 `407cc7ac2105d5d0c5c8ce4c90775adcb7733957157c2dad6aaa0af05f1ebfb1`), with a provenance/limitations README. Exclude its final 14 projection days from completed-training comparisons. Its FTP settings are not authoritative. This exception changes no calculation, source precedence, acceptance criterion or execution gate.
