# P5-01 — Rolling Training Advisor: Home, Plan and Workout Context

**Status:** Pending implementation. Owner-directed product direction recorded 2026-10-10.
**Phase:** 5 — Workout intent, outcome and initial planning.
**Dependency:** P4-02 and Phase 4 accepted/merged; current repository is the starting point.

## 1. Purpose

Deliver RideWorks' first useful, explainable **rolling ride recommendation**: today's ride on Home, a dedicated Plan showing every day from today through the **next three projected hard-session days**, and a truthful initial connection between recommendations/intents and completed activities. This is a single-rider, mostly deterministic advisor, not an autonomous AI coach or a fixed seven-day calendar.

## 4. Scope and product rules

### 4.1 Daily recommendations and hard-session rotation

- Use **rider-local dates** and available synced completed activities to establish the actual last hard session, elapsed recovery dates and most recent hard-session types. A prospective recommendation is not an actual workout and must not update history as if ridden.
- Types: **Race**, **Threshold**, **VO2**, **Z2 endurance**, **Easy** and **Recovery**. **NEVER generate Rest/No ride as the day's recommendation.** All projected days have an appropriate *ride* option, even when minimal.
- Following any completed hard training day, propose **day +1 Recovery 45–60 min ~90–100 W**. Day +2 after **Race or Threshold** proposes 45–75 min **105–120 W**, conditional on normal legs; after **VO2**, 45–60 min **100–110 W**. If legs feel heavy, the recommendation becomes Recovery ~90–100 W, and quality work is postponed. The two post-hard recovery dates may be actual easy riding **or assumed rest after current sync**, with no mandated third recovery day.
- The next proposed hard session alternates **Race vs structured** after recent actual hard training. Structured means Threshold or VO2, chosen by a **simple explainable initial heuristic** informed by recent structured type and FTP-improvement objective. This latter subchoice is a working heuristic, **not a requirement to alternate Threshold and VO2 exactly**; do not embed an unapproved escalating interval protocol.
- Normally plan **no more than two recommended hard days in any rolling seven-day date window** (including known actual recent hard days). Actual unplanned races and workouts are accepted as history even if they exceed that target. Do not blindly repeat hard / recovery / easy every three dates, which would imply three hard days in seven.
- On other days, propose Z2 ~60–75 min at 115–120 W where ordinary aerobic riding fits; otherwise Easy ~100–110 W or Recovery ~90–100 W. These watt ranges are **current approximate owner-coach context**, not authoritative historical zones. If dates/FTP or recovery state are inadequate, show the uncertainty instead of manufacturing precision.
- Plan doesn't pick a particular Zwift event, manage `.zwo` files or require individual named workout selections. The rider knows the workout to find in Zwift.

### 4.2 Horizon and as-of semantics

- Show **Today** and daily cards **through and including the third next projected hard-session recommendation**. Count a hard *recommendation* for today if it has not yet been completed. A hard session completed today appears as actual context, **not hard recommendation 1**, so project three *future* hard days after it.
- Fill every intervening calendar date with Recovery/Easy/Z2; a variable multiweek horizon is expected. No fixed "5-day", "7-day" or "next week" truncation. Compute only as far as needed, with a simple defensive limit/failure state rather than silently showing fewer than three hard dates.
- When an unrecorded **past** date follows reliable sync, count it as **assumed rest for planning** (a recovered calendar day), but neither create a completed rest activity nor modify Training State's no-record evidence. An unrecorded **today** isn't yet assumed rest. Projected **future** dates cannot be completed rest.
- A recommended but skipped hard day does **not** count as actual training, make the rider a failure, or reset the two-day recovery sequence. Today's recommendation can be revised tomorrow based on actual activity.
- Home's today's ride and Plan's today must come from the **same state/calculation**, including rider-local date, data freshness and subjective input. On stale/unknown sync, disclose provisional recommendation and avoid confidently treating no-record past days as rest. Avoid circular dependencies on projected future model stress.

### 4.3 Actual-versus-intended behavior

- Use credible source evidence to classify actual hard training, rather than assuming a high selected stress or title alone proves a race or VO2 workout. Independently select sample workouts/races with known labels and compare with true intervals when available; allow a **minimal manual correction** if classification is uncertain. Keep a source/confidence explanation.
- A **recommendation** is not user-confirmed **intent**. Provide a small explicit "I plan to do this" action *if* needed to support an honest intended-versus-actual view; store accepted intent separately from data. Do not silently infer the rider accepted every daily suggestion. A simpler first Activity Review panel may accurately compare actual to a prior **recommendation** if that snapshot is retained and distinctly labeled; do not invent past recommendations retrospectively.
- Activity Review, when evidence is sufficient, should say what actually happened and whether it aligns with intended/recommended stimulus, without treating an unplanned race or skipped easy day as "failed". Absence of confirmed intent should display **no recorded intent** rather than assigning an intent after the fact.
- A minimal user input for **legs normal / unusually heavy** may downgrade the current day and recompute future projections. Do not require a detailed questionnaire. Do not treat today's Fitness/Fatigue/Form as a validated medical readiness diagnosis. If feedback is absent, don't assert legs are known to feel normal; make the hard ride conditional ("if legs feel normal during warmup").

### 4.4 Presentation

- **Home:** prioritize a compact, prominent Next Recommended Ride card in the dashboard's originally intended next-workout area near Recent Activities. Show type, approximate duration/power as useful, an intelligible one- or two-sentence reason and `View Plan`; don't regress existing dashboard cards.
- **Plan:** add nav item **Plan** to existing shell, route and accessible responsive view; day's recommendation at top, then a visibly clear daily rotation ending on **hard day #3**, labeled relative and rider-local dates, hard-day numbering, estimated intensity, source/reason/assumptions, latest actual hard training and sync freshness. A long scroll is fine, no forced one-screen fit.
- **Activity Review:** lightweight recommendation-versus-actual context only with legitimate evidence/intent provenance. Avoid copying private source data into public reports or fixtures.
- No complicated calendar editor, no drag/drop weeks, no third-party scheduler, no AI call for every load, no training-file import.

## 6. Explicit non-goals

No Phase 6 adaptive trainer or autonomous workout progression optimization; no FTP auto-estimation, calorie/weight targets, universal periodization engine, race-event discovery, Zwift `.zwo` file management, syncing back to Zwift, automatic calendar import/export, detailed readiness questionnaires, workout prescriptions that force specific Zwift file names, large speculative source or framework refactor, or changes to Strava API access strategy.

## 7. Required verification

1. **Exact variable-length horizon:** using deterministic histories, verify today through **three next uncompleted hard recommendations**, with intervening dates all shown, including starting on a projected hard date, today already completed a hard ride, recent easy day, skipped day, and already-two-hard-days-in-seven case. Count and last displayed date must be right; never truncate at fixed 3/5/7 days.
2. **Recovery semantics:** race/threshold +1 recovery and +2 easy-to-Z2; VO2 +1 recovery and +2 lower easy; heavy legs -> recovery not rest and postpone hard; no forced third recovery day. A past missing day after confirmed sync counts as *assumed* rest for planning and does not alter underlying no-record source evidence. Today's missing ride and unsynced prior day don't imply rest.
3. **Hard-day balance:** enforce the default race/structured alternation, avoid >2 *recommended* hard days in rolling 7, reflect actual unexpected high-intensity rides, distinguish threshold from VO2 if known and stay honest if ambiguous.
4. **Honesty:** do not call recommendations completed; completed actual differs from recommendation; intent needs explicit rider confirmation if asserted. Classification uncertainty is surfaced and correctable; no implicit HRSS threshold alone labels VO2 or race.
5. **App flow:** Home today's type/reason/power duration equals Plan today; Plan nav and activity context work; desktop/mobile/keyboard/screen-reader basics; no no-ride suggestions anywhere; dates correct across midnight, timezones and fresh/stale sync.
6. **Regression/preservation:** full Python tests; browser checks; stable Activities, Activity Review, Performance, Training State and Home existing features; source/original files and historical FTP untouched; recommended-plan results deterministic on repeated loads. Demonstrate on a private subset including an independently identified structured VO2 session, race and easy/Z2/recovery activities. Keep names/source details local. No need to change source-model eligibility in Phase 4.
7. **Simple behavior review:** manually inspect a real rotation from a known last race/workout through three predicted hard days, with explicit how/why on each significant transition, and show what happens when an extra day is skipped or an unplanned race occurs.
